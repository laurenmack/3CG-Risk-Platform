Cyber risk appraisal · Pittsburgh Regional Transit

Risk tools for PRT's switch to ReadyFare

PRT is moving fare collection from ConnectCard to ReadyFare, a system run by outside vendors. These tools show which vendors carry the most risk, which risks matter most, and what an attack could cost.

The tools

Tool 1

Vendor Scorecard

Rates each fare system vendor on six criteria and sorts them into High, Medium, and Low risk tiers.

How they work together

Step 1

Score the vendors

A High-tier vendor makes its risks more likely.

Step 2

Map the risks

The heat map raises those risks and shows the worst ones.

Step 3

Price the top risks

FAIR puts a dollar range on them.

Step 4

Fix and rescore

Contract and security fixes lower the scores over time.

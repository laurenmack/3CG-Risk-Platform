How to read this scorecard
  • Each vendor is scored on six criteria, such as how sensitive its data is and how much fare collection depends on it.
  • The scores are weighted and combined into one number. Click a tier below to show only those vendors.
  • Missing information counts against a vendor. If we couldn't find proof of security, we scored it as risky until the vendor shows otherwise.
  • Click See why on any vendor for the reason, evidence, and source behind each score.

How we score

Criteria and weights follow NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1, CISA's Secure by Demand guide, and APTA's transit cybersecurity practice. Weights are 3CG judgments and can be changed on the Weights tab of the Google Sheet.

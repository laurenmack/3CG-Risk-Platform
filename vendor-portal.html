Planned feature

Vendor Portal

A future space where PRT's vendors could sign in and submit security evidence, such as certifications, audit reports, and breach contacts, so their scores reflect verified information.

Owner: [Name] · Vendor Portal Planned

What vendors would do

Upload certifications (SOC 2, PCI DSS, ISO 27001), confirm contract terms, and name a breach contact.

What 3CG would do

Review each upload and change its evidence level from Claimed or Unknown to Verified, which can lower the vendor's score.

Before it's built

GitHub Pages can't check passwords or store uploads safely. A real login needs a service such as Google Forms, Firebase, or the university's sign-in.
